# Sell My Stuff

Selling online is a hassle. Let AI price, post and negotiate for you.

When you visit the Sell My Stuff website, you're prompted to upload photos of the item you're selling. AI agents review the photos, ask questions and help you find the best price and selling market to sell the item. With approval, the posting is automatically made for you on the best selling sites.

Convos is used as the private communication medium for Agents to coordinate product research, best language for the selling post and best price given current market data.

Buyer interactions are fully automated – respond to low ball offers, serious buyers and arrange a meeting with the best buyer using Sell My Stuff's agent. You approve the date/time/location to meet and Sell My Stuff will arrange direct communication between yourself and the buyer via a private Convos chat. After the sale, delete the Convos chat and you don't have to interact with the buyer again.

## Tech Stack

- **XMTP**: Secure messaging backbone.
- **Convos (Assistants API)**: Multi-user group chat interfaces and proxy routing for privacy.
- **OpenClaw**: AI agent intelligence, task routing, and automated negotiation logic.
